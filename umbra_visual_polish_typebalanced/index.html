ABOUT UMBRA: BARROW WARD

WE BUILD WORLDS
THAT WATCH BACK.

Umbra: Barrow Ward is an independent creative group focused on developing original projects, primarily horror games. We aim to create unsettling worlds, memorable atmosphere, and experiences that stay with players long after they leave.

Our main project, HitSeek, was founded in 2025 by RawPaw. We continue to grow the team and explore new ideas across game development, art, sound, and storytelling.
